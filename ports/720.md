As far as I can find, the first standalone, JIT based Xbox 360 emulator on Apple Vision Pro, and a sibling to Xbawks, my original Xbox emulator. A port of XeniOS with a visionOS shell: the game on a big screen in your room, JIT through Apparition, Sense or gamepad input. Bring your own games.
